Step-by-step

How each batch is processed

  1. Raw intake & multi-media sediment filter

    Source water is pre-screened to remove suspended matter, silt, and coarse particulate before entering the plant.

  2. Activated carbon filtration

    High-iodine activated carbon beds adsorb residual chlorine, bad odour, chemical residues, and volatile organic compounds.

  3. Industrial Reverse Osmosis (RO)

    Semi-permeable thin-film composite membranes strip dissolved solids (TDS), excess sodium, nitrates, and toxic heavy metals down to safe international limits.

  4. Natural mineral balancing

    Essential dietary minerals (calcium, magnesium, potassium) are precision-dosed to restore optimal physiological pH (6.8–7.4) and crisp, refreshing taste.

  5. Ultrafiltration (UF)

    Sub-micron hollow-fibre filtration captures minute biological contaminants, colloidal silica, and microscopic particles.

  6. High-intensity UV sterilisation

    Germicidal ultraviolet radiation (254nm wavelength) disrupts microbial DNA, guaranteeing 99.99% disinfection without any chemical byproducts.

  7. Ozonation & sterile sealed filling

    Returnable 20L jars are jet-sanitized, ozone-rinsed, automatically filled, and hermetically capped for fresh doorstep delivery.
