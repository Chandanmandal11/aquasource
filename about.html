Purification Process

Seven stages, tested one batch at a time

From intake to hermetically sealed jar, here is how we ensure each drop meets Nepal drinking water standards.

  1. 1. Raw intake & multi-media sediment filter

    Natural source water is pre-screened for heavy particulates and suspended sediment.

  2. 2. Activated carbon absorption

    High-iodine activated carbon strips chlorine, organic compounds, and any foreign odour.

  3. 3. Industrial Reverse Osmosis (RO)

    Semi-permeable high-pressure membranes filter out heavy metals, excess dissolved salts, and micro-particles.

  4. 4. Mineral re-balancing

    Natural calcium, magnesium, and essential trace electrolytes are balanced for optimal taste and healthy TDS levels.

  5. 5. Ultrafiltration (UF)

    Sub-micron hollow-fiber membranes catch microscopic impurities that bypass standard stages.

  6. 6. High-intensity UV sterilisation

    Ultraviolet lamps eradicate bacteria and viruses at the cellular level without chemicals.

  7. 7. Ozonation & hermetic capping

    Water is enriched with pure ozone for prolonged microbial protection, and filled into sanitized jars sealed the exact same day.
